What DEEP Y listens for

Delivery · set by your voice

Speed
The whole reading sits at WPM, the pace the report calls appropriate. No line moves more than a tenth away from it, so nothing lurches. Keep one gear and let the rests do the work.
Pause
seconds of planned silence. The longest stops come after lines . Count them in your head; they always feel too long to the speaker.
Rhythm
Sentences run from words, so the beat keeps changing. Do not smooth it out.
Empathy
The voice drops in pitch and volume from line 7 to line 16, line 12 is acted in her voice, and line 17 snaps back up. A calm, even reading loses the most points here.

Accuracy · set by your pronunciation

Phonemes and words
Standard Southern British. Drill these on their own first: condemned, birthmark, evidence, screenshot, actually. Keep one accent from the first word to the last.
Syllable stress
Underlined words carry the stress. Make them louder, longer and higher than the words around them.
Intonation
DEEP Y compares your rise and fall with an AI reading, word by word — so the curve under every line is exactly what you are being measured against. It runs left to right across the words: above Flat is higher than this voice's middle, below it is lower. Shaded columns are the stressed words, and the column lights up as the word is spoken. Copy the shape, not only the words, and mind the arrow at the end: only line 1 rises. Record a line and your own curve is drawn over it as a dashed line, measured the same way, so a lower voice is no disadvantage — only a flatter one is.

How to practise

  1. Listen once with your eyes on the script. Do not speak yet.
  2. Switch to Repeat after me. Copy each line in the gap, matching the speed and the melody, not only the words.
  3. Press Record this line on a line you find hard. Your own melody is drawn over the guide's as a dashed line, and any word that moves the wrong way is marked in red. Try the line again until the two curves sit on top of each other.
  4. When a take sounds right, press Save this take and send the file to your teacher.
  5. Record the whole speech with a stopwatch. Finish between 1:50 and 1:58. The recording must stay under 2:00.
